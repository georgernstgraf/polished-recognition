# Was funktioniert

Kurzer, nutzersichtbarer Stand der App — bei jedem Issue-Abschluss um einen Satz
ergänzt (neuestes oben), vom Agenten gepflegt, nie vom Owner.

- Discarded cross-app dictations land in the Workbench automatically — one toast
  confirms it; an Advanced checkbox turns the feature off (then every discard says so
  instead). (2026-11-28, #219)
- Toasts appear above the lower third of the screen — over the keyboard and in the
  Workbench — instead of hugging the bottom edge. (2026-11-28, #219)
- A paused take now shows ‚Paused…' with a pause icon instead of ‚Listening…' and the
  mic symbol. (2026-11-28, #218)
- Entering another app while a take is paused no longer stalls the keyboard — the take
  is secured in the Workbench and a fresh recording starts immediately. (2026-11-27, #217)
