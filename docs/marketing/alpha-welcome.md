## Google Group welcome message

*(Paste into the group's "Welcome message" field.)*

Welcome! This group gives you early access to **Polished Recognition** — the
open-source voice keyboard for Android — through Google Play's alpha track.

**Getting access takes two steps, and both must use the same Google account:**

1. **Join this group** (you're here — step done).
2. **Accept the tester invite:**
   https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.georgernstgraf.polishedrecognition

Once accepted, install or update Polished Recognition from the Play link on
that page. Your Play status must read **Member**, not *Pending* — only then do
you receive the alpha builds.

**Please stay opted in for 14 days continuously.** Google requires 12+ testers
staying opted in for two weeks before an app can be released to everyone —
your continuous participation is what unlocks the public release.

Found a bug or have an idea? Please open an issue:
https://github.com/georgernstgraf/polished-recognition/issues
